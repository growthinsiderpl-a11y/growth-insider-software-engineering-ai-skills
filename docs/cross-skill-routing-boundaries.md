# Cross-Skill Routing Boundaries

Verification date: 2026-09-03

These three Growth Insider Skills may be installed together. Route by the binding decision, not by the keyword that appears first.

## Primary ownership

### Marketing & Growth (`growth-insider-marketing-growth-ai-skills`)

Owns market demand, positioning, acquisition, campaigns, content distribution, SEO strategy, marketing channels, marketing economics, and marketing lifecycle.

### Software Engineering (`growth-insider-software-engineering-ai-skills`)

Owns code, architecture, implementation, technical reliability, software delivery, technical performance, engineering quality, and production systems.

### Product Strategy & Delivery (`growth-insider-product-strategy-delivery-ai-skills`)

Owns product outcomes, product discovery, roadmap, prioritization, product metrics, requirements, user value, and product delivery governance.

## Collision rules

| Topic | Primary | Optional secondary | Prohibited default |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEO keyword/content/organic strategy | Marketing | Product if page value/product experience is the question | Engineering as primary for content strategy |
| SEO crawl/render/schema code/performance remediation | Engineering | Marketing for intent framing | Marketing as primary for code fixes |
| Landing page message/offer/conversion hypothesis | Marketing | Product if core question is product value | Engineering as primary for copy |
| Landing page implementation/components/performance | Engineering | Marketing for message constraints | Product as primary for CSS/code |
| Pricing as acquisition offer/positioning | Marketing | Product for packaging constraints | Engineering as primary |
| Pricing as packaging/feature gates/monetization design | Product | Marketing for market framing | Engineering as primary |
| Billing implementation | Engineering | Product for policy/rules | Marketing as primary |
| UX discovery/usability/journey | Product | Marketing if acquisition journey only | Engineering as primary for research |
| UI implementation/design-system code/a11y implementation | Engineering | Product for interaction intent | Marketing as primary |
| Acquisition growth | Marketing | Product if PLG loop | Engineering as primary |
| Product-led retention/adoption | Product | Marketing for lifecycle messaging | Engineering as primary for strategy |
| Performance/scalability implementation | Engineering | Product for priority/scope | Marketing as primary |

## Compound sequence

1. Product defines the problem and success criteria.
2. Marketing validates positioning/demand when relevant.
3. Engineering implements.
4. Product measures outcome.

Do not solve ambiguity by activating all three Skills by default.
